Auto Clicker - Auto Macro

Build a macro once, then let your Android device repeat the taps, swipes and screen checks for you.

What it does

  • Tap, long press and swipe at the positions you choose, with optional random offset and delay.
  • Wait for an image, a word or a number to appear on screen before continuing.
  • Branch with IF conditions, loops, variables and timers.
  • Edit macros in a floating editor on top of the app you are automating.
  • Export a macro to a file and import it on another device.

Your data stays on your device

The app needs no account. Macros, images and settings are stored on your device only. Screen content is read on the device to run your macro and is not uploaded.
